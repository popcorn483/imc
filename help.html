Help & tutorials

Everything the IMC app can do, step by step, with pictures. No computer knowledge needed: if you can use Facebook, you can use this. Pick a topic on the left, or search at the top.

📋 The hubWhat's on, deals, posts to make, adverts, staff briefings and the foyer TV settings. 🎨 Photo & video editorTurns a film, deal or idea into a finished post, Story, video or poster. 📺 Foyer TV playerPlays trailers, showtimes, deals and adverts on the foyer TVs, all day, on its own.

Your first 10 minutes

Do these once. After that the app looks after itself.

  1. Open the hub and check the cinema in the top right says IMC Enniskillen. Film times, prices and posters come from imc.ie and update by themselves three times a day.
  2. Make text bigger if you like: click Aa at the top. More on this.
  3. Add your own photos once: your LUXX seats (on the LUXX page), and your Candy King pick 'n' mix stand or kiosk (in the editor's Photo tab › Your product photos). Every design uses them from then on.
  4. Set up each foyer PC: follow Set up a foyer PC. It takes about 5 minutes.
  5. Connect the manager's PC so changes reach the foyer TVs by themselves: Changes from another PC.
  6. Try Easy mode: on the hub's Today page click Make an ad step by step › Start and follow the questions.
💾 Where your work is saved: everything you make or change is saved automatically in this browser on this computer, as you go. There's no Save button to forget. To keep a copy, or to move your posts to another computer, use Posts › Export backup (see Planner and Posts).

Daily and weekly routine

Every day (about 5 minutes)

  1. Open Manager home. Work down To do today and tick things off.
  2. Make today's best post: click Make it (or Make Post on the Today page).
  3. In the editor, check the words, then Download PNG or Share to Story.
  4. Post it on Facebook or Instagram. Then click Mark posted in Adverts so the planner knows.
  5. Leave a handover note for the next manager if anything happened.

Once a week (about 20 minutes)

  1. On Thursday or Sunday, open Week ahead and click Add every best post to the planner.
  2. Check Deals prices are right.
  3. Look at Foyer trailers › Recommended for new trailers to add.
  4. Print the staff briefing and this week's films for the window.
  5. Copy the weekly report into an email for head office.

Run the cinema

Worked out from imc.ie's listings and running times. Pick the day along the top of each tab.

Open Run the cinema ↗

Screens & changeovers

  • A timeline of every screen through the day. Each block is a showing, from the start of the ads to when the film ends.

Staff briefing

Every film today with its rating, who can watch it, its length, its times and screens, and when each one ends, plus today's offers. Click 🖨 Print briefing and pin it up before the first show.

Staffing guide

How many people each hour needs, based on shows starting and ending. Change the four numbers at the top to match how you work (managers always on, shows per box office person, films ending per usher, fewest people in the building), then Print.

Group prices

For a party or school group: choose the day, tick Kids Club or LUXX if it applies, then type how many adults, children and so on, and how many trays or combos. The total, and a reply you can copy and send, appear on the right. Prices are imc.ie's normal prices; any group discount is up to you.

Weekly report

Showings, films, openings and the busiest day this week, the full programme, and highlights (new films, last chances). Copy as text (for email) puts it ready to paste into an email; Print prints it.

Set up a foyer PC

Each foyer PC runs the IMC Foyer TV app. It has no settings of its own: it always plays what the manager's computer has set, in step with the other TVs, and it starts by itself. Do this once on each foyer PC (about 5 minutes).

Open the IMC Foyer TV app ↗ Foyer trailers settings ↗
  1. On the foyer PC, open Chrome (or Edge). Go to the IMC app's address and add tv.html at the end. The Foyer TVs page in the hub shows the full address with a Copy address button.
  2. Pick the cinema and whether this is a Foyer TV or the LUXX lounge, then click ▶ Start this TV. That click also allows sound.
  3. Install it as an app (recommended): click ⬇ Install as an app on this PC on the set-up screen, or in Chrome choose ⋮ › Cast, save and share › Install page as app. An “IMC Foyer TV” icon appears on the desktop and it opens full screen, with no browser bars.
  4. Make it start when the PC switches on: press Windows key + R, type shell:startup and press Enter. Drag the IMC Foyer TV icon from the desktop into that folder with the right mouse button and choose Create shortcut here.
  5. Leave it running. It updates its listings, picks up the manager's changes within about a minute, installs new versions by itself and keeps its sound.
  6. If you use trailer files from the studios, make a folder called TRAILERS on this PC's desktop (see studio trailers).
Check a TV: press I on the foyer PC. “Settings in use: the manager's (up to date)” means it's following the manager's computer. To change the cinema or screen later, open tv.html?setup.
🔇 After a power cut or restart: the player starts again by itself, but browsers block sound until someone clicks once. Until then, trailers play silently and a small note says “click anywhere to turn it on”. Click once anywhere on the TV picture and the sound comes back.
Sound with no click at all (optional, one-time): right-click the Chrome shortcut on the foyer PC › Properties, and at the end of Target, after the last quote mark, add a space and --autoplay-policy=no-user-gesture-required. Click OK. Always start Chrome on that PC from this shortcut.

Keys on the foyer PC

F full screen · → skip to the next screen · B show the board now · M sound on/off · I information (is it up to date, and following the manager's settings?).

The playlist

At the top of Foyer trailers: exactly what plays on the foyer TVs, in order.

  • Each trailer has a label: IMC.IE (a film from the listings, with its showtimes card before the trailer), Your trailer, Studio, Video (a YouTube video you added), Your card or Priceboard.
  • Move: drag a row by its ⠿ handle, or use ↑ ↓.
  • Take out: ✕. It moves to Taken out underneath; Put back returns it.
  • ⤮ Shuffle now mixes the order once. Shuffle on the TVs every time round mixes it fresh each loop. Reset order goes back to films by next showing.
  • On the foyer TVs now: with the TVs in step, a dark bar above the list shows what's on the TVs at this moment and how long is left, and that row is highlighted in gold with “● On the TVs now”.
  • Show what plays in between lists the board, deals slides, adverts, messages and welcome videos between the trailers, exactly as the TVs will show them.

Add your own card

  1. Click ＋ Add a card above the playlist.
  2. Type a heading (for example “Halloween fancy dress”). The small top line (“This Saturday”) and the lines of text underneath are optional: one line of text per line in the box.
  3. Pick a background colour by clicking a circle.
  4. For a picture, click Choose from IMC images and click one, or paste a picture's web address. No picture takes it off.
  5. Choose how many seconds it stays on. To show it only for a while, set the first day and last day.
  6. Check the picture on the right (“How it looks on the TVs”), then click Save card. It appears in the playlist as Your card; drag it to where you want it. The TVs pick it up within about a minute.

To change or delete a card, click Edit on its row in the playlist. Delete card is at the bottom.

Make a priceboard (shop prices)

A full-screen price list for the TVs. Make a few small boards rather than one big one (for example Deals, Drinks & popcorn, Pic n Mix): fewer items means bigger, easier-to-read text.

  1. Click ＋ Add a priceboard above the playlist.
  2. Type the board name, the big heading (for example “Drinks”).
  3. Under ＋ Add a category, click a button such as Popcorn or Drinks. A category appears with some suggested items: change the names, remove any with ✕, and add more with ＋ Add an item.
  4. Type each price as a number, for example 4.50. The £ sign is added for you. You can also type words such as “2 for £5”.
  5. Sizes: type them separated by commas, e.g. Small, Medium, Large, then click elsewhere. A price box appears for each size. Leave it empty for one price.
  6. Pictures (optional, up to 4): click ＋ Add from our image library, pick a tab (Food & drink, Deals or IMC) and click each picture you want. Click a picture again to take it off, or use the red ✕ on its thumbnail. ◀ ▶ change the order.
  7. Make pictures bigger or smaller with the Picture size slider, and choose Where they go: on the right, on the left, or along the bottom. Watch the preview as you slide: the prices make room by themselves.
  8. Sound: keep the usual music, pick a different style for this board (Upbeat suits food and deals), or No music. Tick Play a chime as it comes on to make people look up. ▶ Listen lets you hear it.
  9. Pick a background colour, check the preview on the right, and click Save priceboard. It goes into the playlist as Priceboard: drag it where you want it.

Prices changed? Click Edit on the board's row in the playlist, change them and save. The TVs update within about a minute. Make a copy is handy for a second board in the same style.

Add any YouTube video

  1. Click ＋ Add a YouTube video above the playlist.
  2. Paste the video's link (copy it from the address bar, or YouTube's Share button). Its name fills in by itself; change it if you like.
  3. Click Add to the playlist. It's checked first: if it can't play outside YouTube you'll be told. It plays full screen and is labelled Video in the playlist. ✕ takes it out.
Watch a video all the way through before adding it: whatever it shows, the foyer sees.

Which films are included is set under Which films further down: every foyer-safe film in the next 7 days (it updates itself), or only the ones you tick. Films rated 15 or 18 never play; films with no rating yet stay out until you watch the trailer and tick it.

Between the trailers

Which slides play between trailers, and what scrolls along the bottom of the screen.

  • Slides: tick or untick Wednesday Meal Deal, Kids Club, LUXX upgrade, Coming soon posters, Last chance to see and Scan to book. Slides only appear when they apply (Kids Club when there are Kids Club showings, and so on).
  • Bottom strip: tick or untick each line, and change its wording in the box. A line you've changed stays as you typed it; Use the automatic wording puts it back.
  • Your own messages (for example “Halloween fancy dress on Saturday!”) go in the box under Screens between trailers. Each gets its own slide and scrolls along the strip.
  • Also under Screens between trailers: how often the Now showing board appears, deals slides on or off, the info strip, and screen numbers.

Recommended trailers

The newest public trailers from the film distributors' official YouTube channels (Warner Bros., Disney, Universal, Sony, Paramount, Lionsgate, StudioCanal and more), checked three times a day. Red-band trailers and Disney+ shows are left out.

  1. Filter by distributor along the top, or tick Only films on imc.ie.
  2. Click the picture or ▶ Watch to watch it first, in a pop-up.
  3. Add to loop adds it with its release date: it plays as “In cinemas …” until the film opens, then stops. Use as its trailer (for films already on imc.ie) swaps in this trailer for that film.
  4. Changed your mind? Remove next to “Added ✓”. Don't want to see it? Ignore (and “Show ignored” brings it back).
  5. Rating: shown on each card. If it's not known or is wrong, choose it in the Rating box. Trailers rated 15 or 18 can't be added.
US dates: Disney, Paramount and Lionsgate cards come from American channels, so their dates say US release. Check the UK date and change it in Your own trailers.

Trailer files on the foyer PCs (Google Drive sync, the easy way)

Google Drive keeps an IMC TRAILERS folder copied onto every foyer PC by itself. You add trailer files on the manager's PC; a few minutes later they're on the foyer PCs, and the TVs play them from there instead of YouTube: full quality, no buffering, even with no internet.

Once on the manager's PC

  1. Install Google Drive for desktop and sign in with the cinema's Google account. In File Explorer open Google Drive → My Drive and make a folder called IMC TRAILERS.

Once on each foyer PC: the one-file setup (easiest)

⬇ Download the IMC Foyer PC setup

  1. On the foyer PC, download the file above (or copy it over on a USB stick) and double-click it. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway. When it asks “Do you want to allow this app to make changes?”, click Yes.
  2. It installs Google Drive for desktop (if it isn't there already) and sets it to start with Windows.
  3. A Google window opens: sign in with the cinema's Google account (the one with the IMC TRAILERS folder). This is the only thing you type; the setup never sees the password.
  4. It makes C:\IMC TRAILERS, a real copy of the Drive folder on this PC, and keeps it up to date every 10 minutes by itself: new trailers are copied in, deleted ones are taken out. (No “Mirror” setting needed.)
  5. It asks whether the TV should start by itself, full screen, with sound (no click needed) when the PC switches on. Say Y.
  6. Last, the TV app opens: click 📁 Choose the trailers folder, pick This PC → C: → IMC TRAILERS, choose Allow on every visit, then Start this TV.
Check it's working: the file C:\ProgramData\IMC\sync.log lists every trailer copied or removed. Running the setup again is safe: it just checks everything.

Or by hand

  1. Install Google Drive for desktop and sign in with the same Google account.
  2. Make sure the trailers are really on the PC: click the Drive icon by the clock → ⚙ Settings → Preferences → under Google Drive choose Mirror files. (Or keep “Stream files” and right-click the IMC TRAILERS folder → Offline access → Available offline.)
  3. Open the IMC Foyer TV app's set-up screen: the TV app's address with ?setup on the end, e.g. popcorn483.github.io/imc/tv.html?setup.
  4. Click 📁 Choose the trailers folder, go to Google Drive → My Drive → IMC TRAILERS and click Select folder. When Chrome asks, choose Allow on every visit. Then Start this TV.
  5. Restart the PC once to check: the TVs should start as normal. If Chrome ever asks again, the one click you already make for sound also lets the TV read the folder.

Once in the hub (manager's PC)

  1. Open Foyer trailers → the 🎬 Trailers tab → 📁 Choose the IMC TRAILERS folder on this PC and pick Google Drive → My Drive → IMC TRAILERS. Every file shows the film it plays for and ✓ Will play; change the film in the list if it's wrong. For a film that isn't on imc.ie yet, choose ➕ New film, type its name and opening date: it plays with “In cinemas” and the date until it opens. Films with a file show 📁 Trailer file in the Playlist.

Every time you get a new trailer

  1. Save the MP4 into IMC TRAILERS on the manager's PC, named after the film (e.g. Wicked For Good trailer.mp4).
  2. That's it: Drive copies it to the foyer PCs and the TVs pick it up within half an hour (press I on a TV to see “Trailers folder on this PC”). Delete files from the folder when the film has finished; Drive removes them from the foyer PCs too.

The Trailers tab: every film and where its trailer comes from

  • Foyer TVs → 🎬 Trailers lists the films showing at IMC now (from imc.ie) and the UK releases coming soon (the Film Distributors' Association's release schedule, checked every morning).
  • Each film shows its trailer: ▶ YouTube (streamed) and/or 📁 File (from the IMC TRAILERS folder). A film with a file plays the file. Click a film to watch the trailer, paste a different YouTube link or choose its file.
  • Hide takes a film out of this list without changing the TVs (it keeps playing if it was on). Hidden films are under the Hidden filter, with Unhide.
  • The Playing on foyer TVs switch puts a film on the foyer TVs or takes it off. Films rated 15 or 18 can't go on. A film that isn't rated yet asks you to watch its trailer first.
  • A trailer file that doesn't match any film gets a ⚠ warning at the top: pick its film, or ➕ Create a custom release with its name and date.
  • When a film has opened (3 days on) and isn't on imc.ie, or it has finished at IMC, its trailer comes off the TVs and the hub deletes its file from the IMC TRAILERS folder by itself. Chrome asks once to let the hub change the folder. Deleted files stay in Google Drive's Bin for 30 days.

Downloading straight from the studio sites (WB 360, Disney and others)

  1. On the manager's PC, in Chrome: ⋮ → Settings → Downloads → Location → Change → Google Drive → My Drive → IMC TRAILERS. Every download now lands in the trailers folder by itself. (Or turn on Ask where to save each file if you'd rather choose each time.)
  2. On the studio site, download the MP4 / lobby / web version of the trailer. Don't pick the DCP: that's for the cinema projector, and a foyer PC can't play it.
  3. Leave the studio's file name as it is. Names like WICKED-FOR-GOOD_TLR-3_F_EN-XX.mp4 or WICKEDFORGOOD_TLR2.mp4 get matched to the film automatically. If one isn't, set its film in the hub's 🎬 Trailers tab.
Each TV plays a film's file once it has arrived on that PC; until then it plays the YouTube trailer. The other way below (a key file, no Drive app on the foyer PCs) still works too.

Other way: the trailer key file

Trailer files from Google Drive (no buffering)

Put the proper trailer files (MP4s from WB360, Disney Box and the other distributors) on the manager's PC. The foyer PCs fetch them by themselves, play them instead of the YouTube trailer, in full quality with no buffering (even if the internet drops), and delete them when the film has gone. Nobody needs to touch the foyer PCs.

Set it up once (about 20 minutes, plus one visit to each foyer PC)

  1. Install Google Drive on the manager's PC. Search for “Google Drive for desktop”, download it from Google, install it and sign in with the cinema's Google account. A Google Drive drive appears in File Explorer.
  2. Make the trailers folder. In File Explorer, open Google Drive → My Drive, right-click → New → Folder, and call it IMC TRAILERS. Anything you save or drag into it uploads to Google Drive automatically. (You can also upload files at drive.google.com.)
  3. Copy the folder's link. Go to drive.google.com, open the IMC TRAILERS folder and copy the address from the address bar (you'll paste it into the hub).
  4. Make the trailer key file (a Google “robot account” that can only read the folders you share with it):
    1. Go to console.cloud.google.com and sign in with the cinema's Google account. Agree to the terms if asked.
    2. At the top, click the project picker → New project, call it IMC foyer, click Create, and make sure it's selected.
    3. In the search box at the top type Google Drive API, open it and click Enable.
    4. Open the menu ☰ → IAM & Admin → Service accounts → + Create service account. Name it imc-foyer, click Create and continue, skip the role, then Done.
    5. Click the new account → the Keys tab → Add key → Create new key → JSON → Create. A .json file downloads: that's the trailer key file. Keep it safe (don't email it or post it anywhere); put a copy on a USB stick for the foyer PCs.
  5. Share the folder with the robot. In the hub (next step) the robot's email appears with a Copy email button. At drive.google.com, right-click IMC TRAILERS → Share, paste that email, choose Viewer, untick “Notify people”, and click Share. (The folder does not need to be shared by link.)
  6. Put them into the hub (manager's PC). On Foyer trailers, under Trailer files for the foyer TVs (Google Drive), paste the folder link, click Choose the key file and pick the .json file, then click Save and check the folder. The key stays on this PC only.
  7. Once on each foyer PC (the only visit needed): plug in the USB stick, open the IMC Foyer TV app's set-up screen (the TV app address with ?setup on the end, e.g. popcorn483.github.io/imc/tv.html?setup), click Add the trailer key file, pick the .json file, then Start this TV. Take the USB stick away. From then on that PC fetches the trailers by itself.

Every time you get a new trailer

  1. Save the MP4 into the IMC TRAILERS folder on the manager's PC. Name it after the film, e.g. Wicked For Good trailer.mp4. Extra words like “trailer 2”, “final” or “UK HD” are fine.
  2. Give Google a few minutes to upload and process it, then on Foyer trailers click Save and check the folder. Each file shows the film it will play for and ✓ Will play. If the match is wrong or missing, choose the right film from the list (or Don't use this file).
  3. That's it. The foyer PCs check the folder every half hour, download what they need one file at a time, and use it from then on. Until a file has finished downloading on a TV, that TV plays the YouTube trailer. Press I on a foyer TV to see how many files it has saved.
Tidying up: files are deleted from the foyer PCs automatically when the film has left the listings. You can delete old files from the IMC TRAILERS folder whenever you like; the TVs remove them too. The old way (copying files to a TRAILERS folder on each foyer PC's desktop) isn't needed any more.

Your own trailers (YouTube links)

  2. Set the In cinemas date on each one so it stops when the film opens.
  3. Move them with ↑ ↓, or Remove. What plays on the TVs can be “Films from imc.ie, then my trailers” or “Only my trailers”.

Studio trailers (WB360, Disney Box and others)

  1. On the foyer PC, download the trailer from the studio's site. Choose the MP4 (H.264) HD version. Save it in the TRAILERS folder on the desktop.
  2. Still on the foyer PC, open the hub › Foyer trailers › Studio trailers. Pick the film (or “Not on imc.ie yet” and type its name).
  3. Click Choose video file and pick it from the TRAILERS folder. It's checked to make sure it plays.
  4. Optional: Stop playing after a date.
📁 Important: trailer files only play on the computer they were added on. Always add them on the foyer PC, from its TRAILERS folder. A download link (for example a Dropbox share link) works on every foyer PC instead.
Passwords for WB360, Disney Box and other sites aren't kept in the app. When you sign in, let Chrome or Edge save the password. Trailer and poster sites on the Foyer trailers page keeps quick links to them.

All foyer TVs in step

With more than one foyer TV, they all show the same trailer, card or slide at the same moment, like one big screen.

  • It's on by default: Foyer trailers › Player › Keep all foyer TVs in step.
  • A TV that's switched on later (or after a power cut) joins in step: if a trailer is part-way through, it shows the Now showing board until the next item starts, then joins exactly in time.
  • Changes you make reach every TV at the same moment.
  • If one TV can't play a trailer (for example its internet is down), it shows the Now showing board for that slot, then carries on in step.

If the TVs ever drift apart: on the manager's PC, open Foyer trailers and click ⟳ Sync all TVs now (above the playlist). Within about a minute every TV reloads and restarts at exactly the same moment. The button shows the time it will happen.

To keep them in step: the TVs use the website's clock, so a PC clock that's a little out doesn't matter. If you use trailer files, add the same file on each foyer PC.

Music

Soft background music under the showtimes cards, board and slides. It fades out when a trailer starts.

  1. Foyer trailers › Player › Music under the cards and slides: Cinematic (calm), Chilled, Upbeat or No music.
  2. Set the Music volume and click ▶ Listen to hear it.

The music is made by the player itself, so it isn't anyone's recording. It needs Sound on.

Changes from another PC

If you edit on the manager's PC and the TVs run on other PCs, connect once and every change reaches every foyer TV within about a minute and a half. No need to touch the foyer PCs.

  1. On the manager's PC: Foyer trailers › Connect (at the top).
  2. Follow the steps in the box to make a GitHub key: Token name IMC hub, Expiration 1 year, Repository access only popcorn483/imc, Contents: Read and write.
  3. Paste the key and click Connect. A green dot means it's working.
If the dot turns red, the key has probably expired: click Change key and make a new one the same way. Disconnect removes it from this computer.

Only connect on the manager's PC: anyone using that computer's browser could use the key to change the app.

No internet

The foyer TVs keep going.

  • Each foyer PC keeps its own copy of the player, the listings and every picture it has shown, so it still opens and runs after a restart with no internet.
  • YouTube trailers are skipped (no black screens). Showtimes cards, the board, slides, welcome videos and trailer files saved on that PC carry on.
  • A small note in the corner says “No internet: showing saved listings”. When the internet comes back, it returns to normal by itself.

Problems and fixes

The foyer TV has no sound

Click once anywhere on the TV picture (after a restart, browsers need one click). Check the TV's own volume, and that Sound on is ticked on the Foyer trailers page. Press M in case it was muted. For sound without ever clicking, see the tip in Set up a foyer PC.

Trailers buffer or look blurry at the start

YouTube still lowers the picture quality when the internet is slow, so a network cable to the foyer PC (instead of wi-fi) helps most. Trailers added as studio files play from the PC itself, in full quality.

Two foyer TVs aren't showing the same thing

Press I on each foyer PC and compare the Running order code (four letters and numbers). If the codes are different, one TV has older listings or settings: click Sync all TVs now on the Foyer trailers page and wait a minute. If the codes match but one TV is a few seconds behind, it catches itself up at the next trailer. If a TV says “this PC's own” settings, set it up with the IMC Foyer TV app (tv.html).

My changes haven't reached the foyer TV

Press I on the foyer PC: a box shows whether it has the manager's settings, when it last checked, and its clock. If it says “this PC's own” or the player version is old, press F5 once on the foyer PC. Wait about a minute and a half: the TV applies changes between screens. If you edit on a different computer from the TV, check the dot at the top of Foyer trailers is green (see Changes from another PC). If it's grey, click Connect; if it's red, click Change key.

A film or trailer isn't playing on the foyer TV

Look in the Foyer trailers Films list: “Rated 15/18” films never play; “No certificate yet” films need you to watch and tick them; “No trailer on imc.ie” needs a YouTube link pasted. Also check it isn't under Taken out in the playlist.

A studio trailer file won't play on the TVs

Files only play on the computer they were added on. Add it again on the foyer PC, from the TRAILERS folder on its desktop. If it says it won't play in a web browser, download the MP4 (H.264) version from the studio's site.

Times or prices look wrong or old

They come from imc.ie three times a day (early morning, around lunchtime and early evening). If imc.ie has changed something since, it'll update at the next check. Prices on your own deals stay as you typed them: change them on the Deals page.

I can't find a post or design I made

Work is saved in the browser on the computer where you made it. Check you're on the same computer and the same browser (Chrome vs Edge), and the right cinema in the top right. Use Posts › Export backup regularly to keep a copy.

A photo is missing on a design

The photo may have been on another computer. Add it again in the editor's Photo tab, or choose one from IMC images. LUXX photos are managed on the LUXX page.

The app is slow on this computer

Click Aa and tick Less movement. Close other tabs. In the editor, turn off animated effects while you work, and switch them back on before saving a video.

The foyer TV says “No internet: showing saved listings”

The internet is down at the cinema. The TV carries on with saved listings and skips YouTube trailers. It goes back to normal by itself when the internet returns: nothing to do.

Something looks broken on the foyer TV

Press F5 on the foyer PC to reload it (it starts again by itself), then click once for sound. It also recovers by itself if it ever gets stuck for more than 7 minutes.
