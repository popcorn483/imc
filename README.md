# IMC Enniskillen Hub

The marketing hub for **IMC Cinema Enniskillen**. It pulls today's film times, coming-soon films, Kids Club and LUXX showings straight from imc.ie. It turns any film or kiosk deal into a still, animated or video graphic in one click, and it plans and tracks every post.

- **Live site:** https://popcorn483.github.io/imc/
- **Photo & video editor:** https://popcorn483.github.io/imc/editor.html

There's no account or server to run. GitHub Pages hosts the site, and a GitHub Action keeps the imc.ie listings fresh.

## Photo & video editor

- **Background:** your photo or video, an official poster (**Whole poster** shows the full poster over a blurred copy of itself), or an IMC background (IMC stage, Sky, Midnight, Sunset).
- **Your media:** everything you upload is kept to reuse, including your current Facebook graphics and videos. **From imc.ie** shows the latest posters and banners.
- **Frames:** IMC brand bar, Premiere, Neon glow, Clean, Cinema ticket, Film strip, Marquee lights, Photo border.
- **Badges:** Coming Soon, New Release, LUXX, Premier, Now Showing, Book Now, release date, Tonight, corner sash, Kids Club, price burst, Selling Fast.
- **Deal pieces:** price ticket, "What's included" card, Save ribbon.
- **IMC images:** IMC's own cinema photos and graphics from imc.ie, and IMC's product and deal photos from the booking pages (AI-upscaled 4× for sharpness by the *Upscale product photos* job), under Photo → IMC images, as a background or placed on the design. Deals and video clips always picture IMC's real product photos; only the gold ticket is a graphic.
- **Food, drink & tickets (drawn, for custom designs):** the IMC ♥ movies popcorn box, drink, the red kids tray with popcorn and a juice bottle, nachos, hot dog, sweet bag, pick 'n' mix, scooped ice cream, ice cream tub and a cinema ticket that shows how many tickets the deal includes. They're drawn in code, and every colour and label can be changed.
- **Film times:** a panel listing films and times, filled from imc.ie and editable.
- **Motion:** slow zoom, light rays, spotlights, bokeh, marquee chase, gold sparkle, popcorn burst, fizz bubbles, confetti, snow, old projector.
- **Per-item animation:** Pop in, Slam in, Slide up, Zoom in, Fade in, Drop & bounce, Spin in, Flip in, Type on (text), Shine, Pulse, Heartbeat, Glow, Swing, Wiggle, Shake, Float or Blink, each with a start time so items arrive in sequence.
- **Seasons & occasions:** one-click Halloween, fireworks night, midterm, Black Friday, Christmas, New Year, Valentine's, St Patrick's Day, Mother's Day, Easter, Father's Day, summer and autumn looks, each with its own background, colours, animation and banner.
- **Seasonal effects:** bats, spooky fog, fairy lights, floating hearts, shamrocks, autumn leaves, fireworks, bunting, twinkling stars, light leaks, glitter, balloons, rain, camera flashes, a neon border, floating embers and a 3-2-1 film countdown intro, with an effect strength slider and effect colour picker.
- **Sizes:** feed, Story, foyer 16:9, Facebook event and cover, and Print A4/A3 (portrait and landscape).
- **Export:**
  - **PNG/JPG** for posts.
  - **GIF** for Facebook, WhatsApp and X.
  - **Video** for Reels, Stories and TikTok. Chrome, Edge and Safari save MP4; other browsers save WebM.
  - **PDF** for printing posters and flyers (A4, scales to A3).
  - **Copy image** to paste straight into a post.

Everything can be dragged, resized, rotated, recoloured and re-ordered. You can also undo and redo, and save your own layouts.

Shortcuts: Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo, Delete removes, Ctrl+D duplicates, arrow keys nudge (Shift for bigger steps).

## How the imc.ie import works

`.github/workflows/import-imc.yml` runs `tools/import-imc.mjs` three times a day. It reads these imc.ie pages for every IMC cinema (pick one with the **Cinema** menu on the Today page):
- today's film times and the next week of dates
- advance bookings
- Kids Club
- LUXX
- the cinema page's banners

It also looks up each trailer's title through YouTube's oEmbed service into `data/trailers.json`, so red-band trailers can be kept off the foyer TVs. It saves posters and banners into `data/` and writes `data/imc.json` for Enniskillen and `data/cinemas/<cinema>.json` for the others. `data/cinemas.json` lists which imc.ie address worked for each cinema and whether listings came in. To import one cinema only, run `ONLY=galway node tools/import-imc.mjs`. To refresh straight away, open **Actions → Import imc.ie listings → Run workflow**.

The raw pages are kept in `data/raw/` so the importer can be adjusted if imc.ie changes its layout. Check `log` in `data/imc.json` to see what each run found.

## Where things are saved

Posts, deals, layouts, your uploaded media and your current design are saved **in the browser you use**. Use **Posts → Export backup** to move posts and deals to another computer or colleague. The imc.ie listings are shared for everyone.

## Images

Use official posters and trailer stills, your own photos and videos from the cinema, or the built-in graphics. The food and drink pictures are drawn in code as illustrations. The app flags any post marked as using an AI-generated image.

## Logo

`imc-logo.png` is used on every frame, logo layer and poster footer. To try a different version on your own computer, use the editor's **Logo** tab.

## LUXX

The hub's **LUXX** page (top bar, or Menu → Every day) is everything for the LUXX recliners:
- **Your LUXX photos:** upload your seat, screen and lounge photos once. They're saved in the browser and shared with the editor (Add tab → LUXX photos). Use "Use first" to choose the lead photo. To use the cinema's Facebook photos, download them from Facebook (⋯ → Download) and add them here.
- **LUXX ads:** seat hero (with today's LUXX times), Upgrade to LUXX (photo print, what's included, LUXX price from imc.ie), photo collage, This week in LUXX, a 12-second LUXX slideshow Reel, Date night in LUXX, a 16:9 foyer screen and a Facebook cover. Each comes in the sizes that suit it, and every text is editable in the editor.
- **In LUXX this week:** every LUXX film and showtime for the next 7 days, each with a "Watch it in LUXX" post (poster, times, LUXX branding).
- **LUXX captions:** six captions with this cinema's name, films and times filled in, ready to copy.
- Links to the LUXX welcome videos and the LUXX lounge player.

The wording sticks to what IMC says about LUXX ("deluxe recliner seats"). Add any other features you want to promote in the editor.

The importer also saves each cinema's building photo from imc.ie to `data/sites/`. These appear in the editor's IMC images and in the LUXX photos panel as "Cinema building".

## Welcome videos (foyer logo reveals)

The hub's **Welcome videos** page makes "Welcome to IMC" logo reveals for the foyer TVs. There are 10 reveals: spotlight, curtain up, film countdown, lens flare wipe, popcorn burst, starfield zoom, marquee lights, gold particles, film strip and neon sign. You can change the top line, big line, message, background, accent colour, extra effect (sparkle, confetti, snow, bats, hearts, fireworks) and length (6–12 seconds). Each one downloads as a 1920 × 1080 video.

Videos marked "on the foyer TVs" play between trailers on the foyer player: after every trailer, every 2nd, every 3rd, or now and then at random (every 2–4 films). They're drawn live by `ident.js`, which the hub and `foyer.html` share. Because the TV can't see the hub's saved settings, the welcome videos travel inside the foyer player link, so reopen the link after making changes. The downloadable foyer pack includes them too, slotted into `playlist.m3u`.

**Fine-tune** (on the Welcome videos page): logo size and position, words size and position, when the words appear, big-line font (bold condensed, elegant serif, clean), big-line and message colours, and your own two background colours. Fine-tuning goes in the foyer link too, so the TVs match the preview.

### LUXX welcome videos

The IMC / LUXX switch on the Welcome videos page has a LUXX set. It uses a gold LUXX wordmark with "Luxury recliner seating" and serif text, and adds three LUXX reveals: gold lines, gold foil shine and warm glow. Every other reveal works with the LUXX wordmark too, and there are two LUXX backgrounds (LUXX lounge, Leather & gold). Three LUXX starters are included.

The **LUXX lounge player** link (Welcome videos and Foyer trailers pages) is for a TV in or near the LUXX area. It shows only films with LUXX showings, with their LUXX times, and plays only the LUXX welcome videos.

## Timeline in the photo & video editor

Animated designs (Reels, product clips, presets) show a timeline under the canvas, with one bar per layer:
- Drag a bar to move it, or drag its ends to change when it appears and leaves.
- The striped part of a bar is the entrance animation.
- Click or drag along the ruler to jump to a moment.

The selected layer's panel also has **Appears at** and **Leaves at** sliders (in seconds) and an animation speed setting. More loop lengths are available: 5, 12 and 20 seconds.

## Cinema Reels, animated presets and overlays

The editor's **Layouts** tab has **Cinema Reels**: eight ready-made 9:16 videos for Instagram and Facebook Reels, TikTok and Stories. They are a coming-soon teaser (next release and date from imc.ie), this week's films (from imc.ie), snack attack, the LUXX experience, Kids Club, a win-tickets competition, Fright night and Opening night. Each plays in scenes. A pink guide shows the parts of the screen that the Reels buttons and caption cover; it's shown in the editor only and never exported. There are also twelve **Animated presets** that work at any size. Point at a tile to preview it.

New overlays in the Motion tab: spinning film reels, moving film strip, curtain reveal, clapperboard intro, cinematic bars, lens flare sweep, retro VHS, spotlight sweep, falling tickets, popcorn rain and friendly ghosts.

## Your product photos

In the editor's Add tab, **Your product photos** has a slot for each item: Candy King pick 'n' mix, popcorn, drinks, sweets, ice cream, nachos, hot dogs, kids tray, LUXX seats and the kiosk. Upload a photo once and deals and video clips use it for that item at that cinema. Photos on a plain white background are cut out; others appear as framed photo cards. They're saved in the browser (IndexedDB), not uploaded anywhere.

Pick 'n' mix is Candy King: it uses your own photo or an IMC pick 'n' mix photo, and is never shown as another brand (e.g. Maltesers).

## IMC images

The editor's **IMC images** (Photo tab) are IMC's own: cinema photos and graphics from imc.ie, and product and deal photos from the booking pages, gathered from every cinema by the importer into `data/library/index.json`. No stock photos are used.

Product photos in `data/products/hd/` are AI-upscaled copies. `onkt.png` (Kids Tray) has been edited to remove the full popcorn box that stands behind the tray in IMC's photo; the upscaling job never overwrites an existing HD file, so the edit stays.
