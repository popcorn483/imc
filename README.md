# IMC Enniskillen Hub

The marketing hub for **IMC Cinema Enniskillen**. It pulls today's film times, coming-soon films, Kids Club and LUXX showings straight from imc.ie. It turns any film or kiosk deal into a still, animated or video graphic in one click, and it plans and tracks every post.

- **Live site:** https://popcorn483.github.io/imc/
- **Photo & video editor:** https://popcorn483.github.io/imc/editor.html

There's no account or server to run. GitHub Pages hosts the site, and a GitHub Action keeps the imc.ie listings fresh.

## Week ahead and the best post each day

The **Today** page opens with **Best post today**: the one post most worth making today, the time to post it, and why. Two other good ideas sit beside it (folded away on a phone). **Week ahead** (in the menu under Plan & track, and in the top bar) does the same for each of the next 7 days.

The picks come from the real imc.ie listings:

- a big date (Halloween, Bonfire Night, Christmas Eve and Day, New Year's Eve, Valentine's Day, St Patrick's Day) wins on the day
- **Wednesday Meal Deal** at 12:00 every Wednesday, with a heads-up Story on Tuesday evening
- a film's opening day at 16:00, and an "opens tomorrow" post the evening before
- **Kids Club this weekend** at 18:00 on Thursday, with a reminder on Friday and a morning Story at the weekend
- **LUXX recliners** on Friday and Saturday when LUXX has showings
- a **coming-soon** film early in the week
- the current season's post (Fireworks night, Christmas and so on)
- the day's **film times** as a Story

Each idea has buttons to make it (Post, Story or Tall post), **Copy caption** and **+ Planner**, which adds it as a draft with the date and time filled in. **Add every best post to the planner** does all seven days in one go.

## Wednesday Meal Deal

It's always in **Your deals**, added for anyone whose saved list was missing it, and has its own button on the Today page. The price and contents come from imc.ie's booking page (ticket, medium popcorn and medium drink, £12.50 when last checked). Edit it under Deals if anything changes.

## Premium editor tools

- **Scan-to-book QR code** (Add → Scan-to-book QR code): a white card with a QR code and "Scan to book" under it. By default it opens this cinema's film times on imc.ie. You can switch it to the LUXX page, imc.ie, one showing's booking page or your own link. It's made on your computer with no outside service, and it scans in every size. Keep it at least 2.5 cm wide on paper.
- **All sizes in one go** (Video, GIF & more → All sizes in one go): previews the design as a post, tall post, Story and foyer screen, then downloads the ones you tick as one zip file.
- **✓ Check** (top bar, and in Easy mode before saving): checks for leftover placeholder words, text too small for a phone, anything cut off the edge, words hidden behind the Story buttons, a missing "Book at imc.ie", a missing film certificate, a missing logo, too many words, a QR code too small to scan, and video length. Most problems have a **Fix it** button. The red number on the button shows how many checks need attention.

## Reply helper

In the menu under Every day, and on the Today page. Pick the question someone asked in a comment or message and the answer is written for you, using imc.ie's latest listings and prices. You can change any of the words before copying it. The questions covered are:

- what time a film is on (standard and LUXX times listed separately)
- what's on today, tomorrow or the day after
- ticket prices
- the Wednesday Meal Deal
- Kids Club
- LUXX
- when a film comes out
- relaxed screenings
- birthday parties and groups
- complaints
- thank-yous

## Countdown and "this or that" poll posts

- **Countdown:** on any coming-soon film, press **Countdown** for a "3 DAYS TO GO" post with the poster, the film's certificate and the opening date. The number is worked out for you. Week ahead suggests one three days before each opening.
- **This or that poll:** on the Today page. Pick two films showing this week and a question ("Which are you seeing?", "Date night pick?" and others). People vote with ❤️ or 👍. Week ahead suggests one on Sundays, using the two films with the most showings.

Both work as a post, tall post, Story and foyer screen, and come with a caption.

## Printable listings

- **Print this week's films** (Today page, and Week ahead) makes an A4 landscape poster of every film and time for the next seven days. Each film has one row, and its LUXX times are marked in black and gold. Kids Club times are in yellow. A "Scan to book" QR code is at the bottom.
- **🖨️ Print today** (on Showing today) does the same for one day, in bigger print on A4 portrait.

Both shrink themselves to fit on one sheet.

## Phone reminders

**🔔 Remind me on my phone** (Planner and Week ahead) downloads your planned posts for the next two weeks as a calendar file. Open it on your phone or computer and each post becomes an alert at its posting time, with the caption inside. It works with iPhone, Android, Outlook and Google Calendar.

## Foyer screen player (foyer.html)

The foyer player loops through:

- each film's **showtimes card**, then its trailer
- a **"Now showing" board**
- **deals and offers slides**
- welcome videos

An **info strip** runs along the bottom the whole time.

- **Showtimes cards:**
  - the poster, a NEW badge in a film's first week, and tags for LUXX, Kids Club, Relaxed and 3D
  - each time shows its screen number, or LUXX for a LUXX showing
  - the next show is highlighted, with a "Next show in 25 min · Screen 7" or "Now seating" chip
  - a **QR code that books that exact showing**
- **During trailers:**
  - the film's name and today's times appear for the first few seconds
  - the booking QR code fades in for the last 25 seconds
- **"Now showing" board:** every remaining show today, with time, certificate, film, screen and a live status:
  - **Now seating** (up to 20 minutes before)
  - **In 35 min**
  - **Book now**
  - **Started**
  
  It fits as many rows as the screen allows and pages through the rest.
- **Deals and offers slides** are made from imc.ie's live data:
  - the **Wednesday Meal Deal** with its price and the popcorn combo photo (Monday to Wednesday, "Today only" on Wednesday)
  - **Kids Club** posters with days and times
  - **LUXX** with the from-price and today's LUXX films
  - **Coming soon** posters with dates
  - **Book on your phone** with a big QR code
  - any **messages you type** in the hub
- **Info strip:**
  - the IMC logo and a clock
  - the next show ("Now seating · Screen 4 · 13:30 Shaun the Sheep")
  - a scrolling ticker with your messages, the Wednesday Meal Deal, Kids Club and LUXX prices, and tonight's last film
- **Opening and closing:**
  - before the first show it says good morning and lists today's films
  - after the last show it says "Thanks for visiting" and shows tomorrow's films and times
- **Reliability:**
  - works on upright (portrait) TVs as well as normal ones
  - keeps the screen awake where the browser allows
  - keeps running from a saved copy if the internet drops, with a small "offline" note
  - reloads itself if anything ever gets stuck for 7 minutes, and refreshes at 5am
  - on very slow PCs it turns off the heavy effects automatically
- **Keys:** → skip · B jump to the board · F full screen · M sound on/off

Set it up in the hub under **Foyer trailers → Screens between trailers**:

- how often the board appears (or off)
- deals slides on or off
- info strip on or off
- whether to show screen numbers
- up to six of your own messages

All of it travels in the TV link, so just copy the link again after changing anything.

## Screens & run sheet (Run the cinema)

In the menu under **Run the cinema**. For today or any of the next six days it shows:

- **Summary:** the number of showings, the first show, roughly when the last film finishes, the most screens running at once, and how many tight changeovers there are.
- **Screen timeline:** one row per screen with a bar for every show. The lighter start of each bar is ads and trailers, and you can set how long those last (10–30 minutes). A red line marks now, and finished shows fade out. LUXX shows are navy and Kids Club shows orange.
- **Changeovers to watch:** where the next show in a screen starts less than 25 minutes after the film before it ends. Under 15 minutes is red.
- **Busy times this week:** a heatmap of how many screens are running each hour for the next 7 days, for planning staff rotas.
- **🖨️ Print run sheet:** an A4 list of every show with:
  - start (doors and ads), screen, film and tags
  - when the film starts and ends
  - the next show in that screen and the changeover time
  - a tick column for staff

End times come from imc.ie's running times. Where one is missing, 2 hours is assumed and marked with ~.

The **Run the cinema** page (menu, under Run the cinema) has five tabs:

## Last chance to see

A film counts as leaving when its last listed showing is within four days and imc.ie's published listings carry on for at least two days after it. Advance bookings for single later shows don't count.

- What's on shows a red **Last chance · ends Sun** chip and a **Last chance post** button. The editor's design has a LAST CHANCE stamp and the last show date.
- Week ahead suggests a last-chance post the evening before the final day.
- The foyer player adds a **Last chance to see** slide with the posters and last show days.
- The staff briefing and weekly report list them.

## How the imc.ie import works

`.github/workflows/import-imc.yml` runs `tools/import-imc.mjs` three times a day. It reads these imc.ie pages for every IMC cinema (pick one with the **Cinema** menu on the Today page):
- today's film times and the next week of dates
- advance bookings
- Kids Club
- LUXX
- the cinema page's banners

It also looks up each trailer's title through YouTube's oEmbed service into `data/trailers.json`, so red-band trailers can be kept off the foyer TVs. It saves posters and banners into `data/` and writes `data/imc.json` for Enniskillen and `data/cinemas/<cinema>.json` for the others. `data/cinemas.json` lists which imc.ie address worked for each cinema and whether listings came in. To import one cinema only, run `ONLY=galway node tools/import-imc.mjs`. To refresh straight away, open **Actions → Import imc.ie listings → Run workflow**.

The raw pages are kept in `data/raw/` so the importer can be adjusted if imc.ie changes its layout. Check `log` in `data/imc.json` to see what each run found.

## Where things are saved

Posts, deals, layouts, your uploaded media and your current design are saved **in the browser you use**. Use **Posts → Export backup** to move posts and deals to another computer or colleague. The imc.ie listings are shared for everyone.

Use official posters and trailer stills, your own photos and videos from the cinema, or the built-in graphics. Food and drink pictures are IMC's own product photos or your uploads. The app flags any post marked as using an AI-generated image.

`imc-logo.png` is used on every frame, logo layer and poster footer. To try a different version on your own computer, use the editor's **Logo** tab.

## Film logos and the background layer

**Film logo** (editor → Add, top section; also in Easy mode for film designs):

## Deleting library pictures

In the editor's Photo tool, every picture in **Your media**, **IMC images** and **From imc.ie** has a ✕ (shown when you point at it, always shown on touch screens). It asks first.
- **Your media:** the picture is deleted from the computer for good.
- **IMC images and imc.ie pictures:** they stay deleted on that computer, even though the daily imc.ie update brings them back into the data. "Bring them back" at the bottom of each list restores them.

## Easy mode (step by step)

For anyone not used to design tools. **✨ Easy mode** (top bar, or the big "Make an ad step by step" button on Today) asks one question at a time, with big buttons:
1. **What's it for?** A film on now, film times for a day, Kids Club, a deal, a coming-soon film, LUXX, a special occasion, or a foyer welcome video.
2. **Which one?** Film posters, days, deals with prices, Kids Club weekend or week, LUXX ad types, occasions.
3. **Where will it go?** Facebook/Instagram post, tall post, Story/Reel, foyer TV, poster to print.
4. **Still or moving?** Only asked for deals and Kids Club Stories.
5. **Ready:** a summary, then "Make my ad".

The editor then opens in its **easy view**, with the design and three steps:
1. **Check the words:** every headline, price and line is a labelled box.
2. **Photo (optional).**
3. **Save or share:** save picture, save video, share to the Facebook story, copy.

"Full editor" switches to all the tools, and "✨ Easy mode" in the editor switches back. The editor remembers which you used last.

## The editor on a computer

- **Tools down the left**, with labels: Designs, Photo, Add, Frame, Animate, Logo.
- **Folding sections:** each section folds away, and only the main ones start open. Click a heading to open or close it. The editor remembers what you open.
- **Around the design:** a **?** button explains how to edit. **⏱ Timings** shows the timeline for animated designs.

**Built for slow PCs:**
- The preview is drawn at screen size, not full size.
- Thumbnails are only drawn for the tool that's open, a few at a time.
- Effect previews only animate when you point at them.
- Weaker computers (4 cores or 4 GB or less) get a lower preview frame rate.

Downloads, videos and GIFs are always full quality.

## The editor on a phone

On phones the editor works like an app:
- **Top bar:** one line with back, size, undo/redo, Video ▾ (video, GIF, JPG, PDF, copy), Story and Save.
- **Your design:** always in view.
- **Bottom tabs:** Edit, Photo, Add, Layouts, Motion, Frame, Layers, Timing and Logo. Each opens a pull-up panel under the design; tap the tab again or **Done** to close it.
- **Tapping something on the design** opens its Edit panel. A dot on Edit shows something is selected.

## Film times posts

## Your own trailer links

On the Foyer trailers page, any film with no trailer on imc.ie (or whose trailer is gone from YouTube or is red-band) gets a box to paste a YouTube link, plus a "Find on YouTube" search. Pasted links are checked:
- the link must be a real YouTube video that can play outside YouTube
- red-band or uncensored titles are refused
- the certificate rules still apply

Your trailer then plays on the foyer player (it travels in the TV link) and goes in the pack's trailer list. "Remove my link" undoes it.

## Foyer playlist

The **Playlist** at the top of the Foyer trailers page lists everything playing on the foyer TVs, in order: films from imc.ie (each gets its showtimes card first), your own trailers and studio trailers, each with a small label.

- Drag a row (or use ↑ ↓) to move it, and ✕ to take it out. Trailers you take out go under **Taken out**, where **Put back** returns them.
- **Shuffle now** mixes up the order once. **Shuffle on the TVs every time round** makes the foyer player shuffle each loop. **Reset order** goes back to films by next showing, then your trailers.
- The order, the trailers taken out and the shuffle setting are sent to the foyer player in its address (`ord`, `hide`, `shuf`), so reopen or re-copy the player link after changing them. New films that turn up later join the end of the list.

## Recommended trailers (new releases from distributors)

The **Recommended: new trailers** section on the Foyer trailers page lists the newest public trailers from the distributors' official UK YouTube channels: Warner Bros., Disney (its main channel, @disney, strictly filtered: only trailers that say they're in cinemas or theaters, and nothing mentioning Disney+ or streaming), Universal, Sony Pictures, Paramount (@paramountpictures), StudioCanal, Lionsgate (@lionsgatemovies), Searchlight, Pathé, Altitude and Vertigo.

- The importer (`tools/recommended.mjs`, run with each import) reads each channel's public feed, its Videos tab and its trailer search. It looks up each new trailer's upload date and description once, and keeps earlier finds for 150 days. The results go in `data/recommended.json`. To add a distributor, add its name and YouTube @handle (or channel id) to `DISTRIBUTORS`.
- It leaves out red-band trailers, clips, featurettes, reviews and digital or streaming releases. The release date ("In cinemas 21 November") and the rating ("Rated 12A") are read from the trailer's description when the distributor gives them.
- Disney, Paramount and Lionsgate use their main (US) channels, so their dates are labelled **US release**: check the UK date before relying on it.
- Each card shows the rating: imc.ie's certificate if the film is listed there, otherwise the BBFC rating from the trailer's description, or "not rated yet". Films rated too high for the foyer can't be added. If the rating isn't known or is wrong, set it yourself with the **Rating** picker on the card or in the pop-up (Auto goes back to the automatic one). A trailer already in the loop is taken off the foyer TVs if you set it to 15 or 18.
- **Watch** plays the trailer in a pop-up on the page before you add it. **Add to loop** puts it in Your own trailers with its release date: it plays on the foyer TVs labelled "In cinemas …" until the film opens. **Use as its trailer** sets it as the trailer for a film already on imc.ie. **Ignore** hides it for good, and "Show ignored" brings it back.
- Your own trailers each have an **In cinemas** date. A trailer stops playing once its date has passed.

## Studio trailers (WB360, Disney Box and other portals)

In **Foyer trailers**, the **Studio trailers** panel takes trailers downloaded from the studios' own sites:

- Pick the film (or "Not on imc.ie yet" and type its name), then **Choose video file** (MP4/H.264, WebM or a browser-playable MOV) or paste a **download link** (a Dropbox share link works; it's turned into a direct link). Each one is checked to make sure it plays in a web browser first.
- A studio trailer replaces that film's YouTube trailer on the foyer player and plays even when the internet is down. Trailers for films not on imc.ie yet play after the films, labelled "Coming soon". An optional "Stop playing after" date takes it off automatically.
- **Video files are kept in the browser on that computer** (IndexedDB), so add them on the PC that runs the foyer TV. Links go into the foyer player's address, so they work on every foyer PC.
- The usual foyer rules still apply: films rated 15 or 18 stay off, and anything that looks red-band is left out.

**Trailer and poster sites** keeps quick links (WB360 by default; add Disney Box and others). The app never stores portal usernames or passwords: it's a public website, so anything saved in it isn't a safe place for them. Let the browser's password manager remember them instead. Downloaded posters and artwork go in the editor's **Photo › Your media**.

## IMC images

The editor's **IMC images** (Photo tab) are IMC's own: cinema photos and graphics from imc.ie, and product and deal photos from the booking pages, gathered from every cinema by the importer into `data/library/index.json`. No stock photos are used.

Product photos in `data/products/hd/` are AI-upscaled copies. `onkt.png` (Kids Tray) has been edited to remove the full popcorn box that stands behind the tray in IMC's photo; the upscaling job never overwrites an existing HD file, so the edit stays.
