Recommended: new trailers

The newest public trailers from the film distributors' official UK YouTube channels, checked three times a day. Red-band trailers are left out. Add one and it plays in the loop with its release date until the film opens. Watch it once first to be sure it's right for the foyer.

Your own trailers

Add any YouTube trailers you want in the loop, for example coming attractions or films not on imc.ie yet. Paste one link, or several at once (one per line). They play in this order, without a showtimes card.

Studio trailers

Trailers from the studios' own sites, like WB360 or Disney Box. Download the trailer there (choose the MP4 (H.264) HD version), then add it to its film here. It plays on the foyer TVs instead of the YouTube trailer, and still plays if the internet drops.

or

Video files are kept on this computer only, so add them on the PC that runs the foyer TV: open this hub on that PC, in the same browser, and add them there. Links work on every foyer PC. Films rated 15 or 18 still stay off the foyer TVs.

Trailer and poster sites

Quick links to the sites you get trailers and posters from. Passwords aren't kept here: when you sign in, let your browser save the password (Chrome and Edge offer to) and it's filled in for you next time. Downloaded posters and artwork go in the photo editor under Photo › Your media, ready for any design.

The pack has a 16:9 showtimes video card and a still for each film, a playlist for TVs that play from a USB stick, and the trailer list. The trailers themselves play through the foyer player from imc.ie's official YouTube links; YouTube doesn't allow them to be downloaded, so for offline trailer files use the ones distributors supply. The safety check works from certificates and trailer titles; nothing listens to the audio, so watch any trailer you're unsure of.
