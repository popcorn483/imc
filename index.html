Your own trailers

Add any YouTube trailers you want in the loop, for example coming attractions or films not on imc.ie yet. Paste one link, or several at once (one per line). They play in this order, without a showtimes card.

The pack has a 16:9 showtimes video card and a still for each film, a playlist for TVs that play from a USB stick, and the trailer list. The trailers themselves play through the foyer player from imc.ie's official YouTube links; YouTube doesn't allow them to be downloaded, so for offline trailer files use the ones distributors supply. The safety check works from certificates and trailer titles; nothing listens to the audio, so watch any trailer you're unsure of.

The foyer player uses every video marked for the TVs, IMC and LUXX. The LUXX lounge player is for a screen in or outside the LUXX area. It shows only films with LUXX showings and their LUXX times, and only your LUXX welcome videos.

The foyer player picks one of the videos marked for the TVs at random each time. They're silent, so the room stays quiet between trailers. If the foyer TV already has the player open, reopen the link (or copy it again from Foyer trailers) to pick up changes.
